# Insurance Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
The Insurance Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI and Excel solution developed to monitor and evaluate branch-level business performance. It provides a centralized view of sales, revenue, meetings, opportunities and individual employee performance, enabling branch heads and the corporate team to make data-driven decisions.

The dashboard combines data from multiple business sources to track new business, cross-sell, renewals, sales targets, client meetings, invoices, and opportunity pipelines.


## Business Objective
The primary objective of this dashboard is to:

- Monitor branch performance against business targets.
- Evaluate individual Account Executive performance.
- Track new business, cross-sell, and renewal achievements.
- Analyze the sales pipeline and conversion ratio.
- Identify top-performing opportunities and revenue-generating accounts.
- Support strategic decision-making through interactive visualizations.


## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel

##  Data Sources
The dashboard integrates data from multiple business reports:

- Individual Target Sheet
- Brokerage & Fees Sheet
- Invoice Sheet
- Meeting Sheet
- Opportunity Report


## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Sales Performance
- New Business Target vs Achievement
- Cross-Sell Target vs Achievement
- Renewal Target vs Achievement
- Achievement Percentage
- Total Revenue

### Individual Performance
- Sales Target by Account Executive
- Achievement by Account Executive
- Number of Invoices
- Meeting Count
- Performance Comparison

### Opportunity Analysis
- Open Opportunities
- Closed Won Opportunities
- Conversion Ratio
- Top 10 Open Opportunities
- Top 10 Won Opportunities

### Sales Funnel
- Revenue by Sales Stage
- Opportunity Pipeline
- Funnel Breakdown


## Dashboard Features

- Executive performance tracking
- Branch-wise business monitoring
- Sales target vs achievement comparison
- Opportunity pipeline analysis
- Revenue funnel visualization
- Meeting tracking
- Interactive drill-down analysis
- Dynamic filtering by Branch and Account Executive
